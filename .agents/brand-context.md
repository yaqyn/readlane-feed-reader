# Brand context

## Brand
- **Name**: Readlane
- **Category**: Local RSS and Atom reader
- **Description**: Collect the feeds you choose and read them at your own pace, in the terminal or a companion web reader.
- **Stage**: Working local application; no hosted deployment or public release yet.
- **Website**: None published.

## Audience
- **Primary audience**: Developers and independent readers comfortable running a local tool. The terminal is the primary product; the browser is a companion.
- **Key problem**: Keeping up with chosen publications without juggling sites or surrendering control of the reading workflow.
- **Their language**: Feeds, sources, posts, reading list. These are product terms inferred from the working app, not user-research quotations.

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: PostgreSQL-backed collection, a keyboard-driven terminal reader, scriptable commands, and a complementary local web reading surface sharing the same data.
- **Adjacent products**: Feedly, Inoreader, and self-hosted feed readers; these are category context, not a researched feature comparison.
- **Market position**: A focused tool for a trusted local environment. No pricing or monetization is established.

## Brand personality
- **Personality words**: Considered, clear, independent, welcoming.
- **Tone**: Plain language with a calm editorial character. Precise about limitations.
- **Voice references**: The owner's maintained workspace writing guidance.

## Values and mission
- **Core values**: Reader control, inspectable behavior, predictable collection, honest scope.
- **Mission**: Keep the publications you choose within reach, on your own schedule.
- **Tagline**: Your feeds. Your pace.

## Goals
- **Primary goal**: Make the working application recognizable and straightforward to install, operate, and read.
- **Key signals**: Successful first run, understandable help, accessible web reading, verified migration and operation.

## Naming scope
Readlane is a product naming decision. A lightweight web search informed the
choice; no domain registration, trademark clearance, or exclusive-name claim
has been made. The new module/repository identity is `readlane`; this checkout
has no remote yet and its workspace directory remains `blog-aggregator`.
